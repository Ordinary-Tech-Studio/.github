# Ordinary Tech Studio

> **Spatial Digital Twins & Organic Semiconductor Intelligence Group**

Ordinary Tech Studio is a frontier deep-tech research initiative bridging flexible organic thermoelectric semiconductors, real-time spatial augmented reality (AR) digital twins, and autonomous neural reasoning agents.

### Core Focus Areas

- **Organic Thermoelectric Semiconductors**: Flexible energy-harvesting nanocomposites (PEDOT:PSS / single-walled carbon nanotubes). Converting ambient and micro-temperature differentials ($\Delta T = 5 \sim 25\,\text{K}$) into sustained micro-power ($S > 180\,\mu\text{V/K}$) for battery-less telemetry.
- **Spatial AR Digital Twins**: Mapping real-world thermal gradients into real-time 3D spatial meshes via WebXR and OpenXR spatial anchors.
- **Autonomous Physics Agents**: Multi-channel physical transport telemetry analyzed and balanced through Claude 3.5 Sonnet tool-calling workflows.
